1. Mobile View (Screen Width: 600px or Less)

  • Product card layout changes from horizontal to vertical.
  • Product image appears at the top.
  • Product details (name, price, description, and button) move below the image.
  • Card width becomes 100% to fit the mobile screen.
  • Padding and font sizes adjust according to the smaller screen size.

2. Tablet View (Screen Width: 601px - 1024px)

  • Product card adjusts according to tablet screen size.
  • Image and details maintain a balanced layout.
  • Card width becomes smaller compared to desktop view.
  • Card width becomes smaller compared to desktop view.
  • Spacing and font sizes are optimized for better readability.

Desktop View (Screen Width: 1025px and Above)

  • Product image and product details appear side-by-side.
  • Card uses horizontal layout.
  • More space is available for content and images.
  • Larger font sizes and spacing provide a better user experience.
